Notchman turns long messages, emails, articles, papers and AI answers into a short spoken TL;DR, keeping every name, date and number. Copy text, tap the Shiba (Dynamic Island on iPhone, floating on Mac and Windows) and listen. Free to start.

**First comment (from the maker):**
Hi Product Hunt! I'm Ritu, and I built Notchman because I kept saving long messages and articles "for later" and never reading them.

Notchman lives where you already are: in the Dynamic Island on iPhone, and as a tiny floating Shiba on Mac and Windows. Copy anything, tap it, and in a few seconds you hear the TL;DR with every key name, date and number. Or have every word read aloud.

- Works over any app: chats, email, PDFs, AI answers, articles
- Pick on screen: draw around text you can't copy
- Free to start; Pro adds long texts and more time with the natural voice

I'd love your feedback, especially on which texts you'd want summarized first. 🐕
