How do I use Notchman on iPhone?

  • Copy a message, article or link.
  • Press and hold the Shiba in your Dynamic Island, then tap TL;DR (or Read for every word).
  • Or share to Notchman from any app's share sheet.

If iOS asks to paste every time, open Settings › Notchman › Paste from Other Apps and choose Allow.

Subscriptions

  • iPhone: manage or cancel in Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Restore on a new iPhone: open Notchman › Go Premium › Restore, signed in with the same Apple Account.
  • Mac and Windows: cancel from the link in your receipt email, or email us.

Privacy and your data

Your history stays on your device. To make a TL;DR and its voice, the text you choose is sent to Groq and ElevenLabs and isn't stored. Read the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service, or email us to access or delete your data.
