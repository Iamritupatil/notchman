Download Notchman

For Windows 10 and 11. Free during the beta.

Download for Windows

Mac version coming soon.

Install

  1. Open Notchman-Setup.exe from your Downloads.
  2. If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway. This appears for new apps during the beta.
  3. Notchman starts in the system tray (bottom right, near the clock) and opens when your computer starts.

Use it

  1. Copy a long message, an AI answer or a link in any app.
  2. Press Alt + T for the TL;DR, or Alt + R to hear it in full.
  3. Change the voice, speed or shortcuts from the tray icon → Settings.
