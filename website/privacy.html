Privacy

Notchman only looks at what you copied, and only when you press its shortcut. It never watches your clipboard, screen or keyboard in the background.

What happens when you press the shortcut

  • The text you copied is read on your computer. If it's a link, your computer opens that page to get its text.
  • For a TL;DR, the text is sent to Notchman's server, which asks Groq's AI to summarize it.
  • The text to be spoken is sent to ElevenLabs to make the voice.
  • Notchman's server does not save the text or the audio. It only counts how much you used each day, against a random ID created on your computer, to keep usage fair. Groq and ElevenLabs process the text under their own privacy policies.

On your computer

  • Recent voice audio is cached so replays are instant. It stays on your computer.
  • Your settings (voice, speed, shortcuts) are saved on your computer.

Don't send what you wouldn't share

Avoid using Notchman on passwords, one-time codes or anything you don't want an AI service to process.
