Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Notchman turns text you choose into a short spoken summary (TL;DR) or reads it aloud. This policy explains what data that involves, why, who helps us, how long it's kept and the rights you have, wherever you live, including under the EU and UK GDPR, US state privacy laws (such as California's CCPA/CPRA) and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

The short version: Notchman only looks at what you choose, when you press TL;DR or Read. It never watches your clipboard, screen or keyboard in the background. We don't sell or share your data for advertising, we don't use ads or trackers, and our server doesn't keep the text you send.

1. Who we are

Notchman is run by Ritu Patil, an individual based in Surat, Gujarat, India ("Notchman", "we", "us"). We are the controller (in India, the "data fiduciary") of the personal data described here. Contact us at ritu@miniclayai.com.

2. What we process and why

  • Text you choose to listen to. When you press TL;DR, the text (or the text of a link, fetched by your device) is sent over an encrypted connection to our server, which asks our AI provider (Groq) to summarize it. To hear it in the Notchman voice, the text to be spoken is sent to our voice provider (ElevenLabs). Purpose: to provide the feature you asked for. Legal basis (GDPR): performance of our contract with you. Our server does not store this text or the audio.
  • A random install ID. Each copy of the app creates a random identifier that isn't linked to your name, email or device identifiers. Our server uses it to count your daily and monthly usage, keep usage fair and apply your plan. Legal basis: contract and our legitimate interest in preventing abuse.
  • Usage counts. Numbers such as "3 TL;DRs today" or "voice characters this month", stored against the install ID or subscription. Daily counts are deleted automatically after about 2 days and monthly counts after about 40 days.
  • Subscription status. If you subscribe, we receive proof of purchase from Apple (iPhone) or our payment provider (Mac and Windows) to know which plan you're on. We don't receive your card details.
  • Technical logs. Our server keeps limited operational logs (time, request type, errors) to keep the service running and secure. They don't contain the text you send. Legal basis: legitimate interests (security and reliability).
  • Support messages. If you email us, we use your email and message to reply.

3. What stays on your device

  • Your listening history, saved audio and settings.
  • On iPhone, your Sign in with Apple details (name and email, if you choose to share them). They are kept in your iPhone's keychain and not sent to our server.
  • On Mac and Windows, "Pick on screen" takes a screenshot and reads its text on your computer; the screenshot is never uploaded. When you click TL;DR or Read on the Shiba, Notchman copies the text you selected (as if you pressed Ctrl+C or ⌘C) and then restores your clipboard.
  • On Mac and Windows, the free voice runs on your computer. The first time it's used, the app downloads the open-source voice model from Hugging Face and supporting files from jsDelivr, which see your IP address like any download.

4. Who helps us (processors)

We share data only with service providers that process it on our behalf and under contract:

  • Amazon Web Services (hosting our server and usage counts, United States).
  • Groq (AI summaries).
  • ElevenLabs (the Notchman voice).
  • Apple (App Store purchases, Sign in with Apple).
  • Our payment provider for Mac and Windows subscriptions, which processes your payment details under its own privacy policy.
  • GitHub (app downloads) and our website host.

These providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where the law requires it (for example for transfers out of the EU, UK or India), we rely on lawful transfer mechanisms such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not "share" it for cross-context behavioural advertising as those terms are defined in California law. We don't use your text to train AI models.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, object to or restrict its processing, and withdraw consent at any time (without affecting earlier processing). Specifically:

  • EU, EEA and UK (GDPR): the rights above, plus the right to complain to your local data protection authority.
  • United States: residents of California and other states with consumer privacy laws (including Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, Texas, Oregon and others) have the right to know, access, correct and delete personal data, to opt out of sale, sharing, targeted advertising and profiling (we do none of these), and not to be discriminated against for using these rights. You can appeal a decision by replying to our answer. We treat Global Privacy Control signals as an opt-out request.
  • India (DPDP Act, 2023): the right to information about your data, to correction, completion, updating and erasure, to grievance redressal, and to nominate someone to exercise your rights if you die or become incapacitated.

Because we don't keep your name or email on our server, most of your data is on your own device, and you can delete it by deleting your history in the app or uninstalling it. For anything else, email ritu@miniclayai.com from the device's app or include your install ID (shown in the app). We answer within one month (45 days where US state law allows), and may need to verify your request.

6. Grievance Officer (India)

Ritu Patil, Grievance Officer, ritu@miniclayai.com, Surat, Gujarat, India. We acknowledge complaints within 48 hours and aim to resolve them within one month. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

7. Children

Notchman is not directed to children under 13, and in the EU/EEA it's for people old enough to consent to online services in their country (up to 16). In India, it is not intended for anyone under 18 without verifiable parental consent. We don't knowingly collect children's data; if you believe a child has used Notchman, contact us and we'll delete what we hold.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, API keys stay on our server, and access to our systems is limited. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you and the authorities of a personal data breach where the law requires.

9. Sensitive content

Don't use Notchman on passwords, one-time codes, health, financial or other sensitive information you don't want an AI service to process. If you do, it's processed only to give you the summary or voice you asked for.

10. Changes

We'll update this policy when our practices change and show the new date at the top. For significant changes we'll also tell you in the app or on this website.
