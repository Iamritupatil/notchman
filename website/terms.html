2. What Notchman does

Notchman makes short spoken summaries ("TL;DRs") of text you choose, and reads text aloud in full. You decide what Notchman processes: it acts only when you press TL;DR or Read, share something to it, or pick text on your screen. Depending on your plan and settings, summaries are written by an AI model and voices are produced by speech services, on our servers or on your device.

The Service is offered in these editions: Free (limited use), Pro and Pro+ (paid subscriptions with monthly allowances of the natural Notchman voice), and the Community edition (source code you run yourself with your own API keys, for personal, non-commercial use only). What each edition includes is described in the app and on our website, and may change as described in section 14.
